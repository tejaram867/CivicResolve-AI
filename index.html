Platform Features

Everything Needed for
Smarter Civic Resolution.

Ten purpose-built capabilities that transform how citizens and governments collaborate on civic issues.

🧠
AI Issue Classification
Our NLP engine automatically reads every complaint, categorises it into the correct civic domain, assigns a severity score and flags it with the right department - all within two seconds of submission, without any human intervention.
🤖 Core AI Engine
🔄
Duplicate Issue Detection
Semantic similarity matching prevents the same pothole from flooding the system. Citizens are redirected to support existing reports instead.
Zero duplicates
🚀
Community Boosting
Citizens upvote issues they've personally witnessed. Boost scores directly influence priority queues, turning collective voice into measurable action.
👥 Crowd-powered
🏛️
Smart Authority Routing
Machine learning maps each issue type to the responsible municipal body and routes it with full context - location, evidence, priority - so no issue lands in the wrong inbox.
⚡ Instant dispatch
📎
Evidence-Based Reporting
Attach photos, videos and GPS coordinates to every complaint. AI validates evidence quality and auto-generates a structured report that authorities can act on immediately.
📍 Geo-verified
📡
Live Issue Tracking
Real-time status updates push directly to citizens. Every stage change - from assignment to resolution - is timestamped and visible.
🔴 Live feed
⚠️
Escalation Workflow
Issues that exceed SLA deadlines automatically escalate to senior officials with a full audit trail, ensuring accountability at every level.
⏱ SLA enforced
🔍
Citizen Verification
Resolution isn't closed until the community confirms the fix. This creates a genuine feedback loop between citizens and authorities.
✓ Verified close
💬
AI Civic Assistant
A conversational AI guides citizens through reporting, answers status queries in natural language and suggests the right category - in multiple Indian languages.
🌐 Multilingual
